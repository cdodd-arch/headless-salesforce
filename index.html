Begin your headless journey

No sales call. No follow-up deck. Four steps.

  1. Open chrome://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked. Select the headless-salesforce folder.
  4. Open any Lightning page. The panel shows in the top right.

It runs on *.lightning.force.com, *.salesforce.com and *.force.com. Full Send changes no records. It breaks the look of your own tab until you reload. Do not Full Send in a client screenshare, unless comedy is the goal.
